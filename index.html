    We check if it works

What we do

Three ways we can help

Not sure which one fits? Tell us your situation in the form below and we'll point you to the right one.

Most common

Already shipped, waiting to get paid

You have a confirmed order and the goods are on their way, but your buyer won't pay for 60–90 days. We arrange for a financing partner to pay you most of that value now, and collect from your buyer later.

  • Fastest option — no long approval process
  • Not a loan against your company; it's an advance on money you're already owed
  • Best for exporters with a real buyer and shipping documents in hand

Ongoing credit for your trade business

You ship regularly and want a standing credit line with a bank or lender, based on your trading history rather than starting from zero each time.

  • Suited to businesses with steady, repeat shipments
  • We prepare your file and introduce you to the right bank

Financing before you ship

You have a confirmed order but need funds to produce or buy the goods first. We help arrange financing against that confirmed order, before anything ships.

  • Needs a real, confirmed order in hand
  • Takes a little longer since production is involved

Common questions

Straight answers, before you ask

How long does the whole process take?

Checking whether your shipment qualifies usually takes a few days. Once matched with a financing partner, funds typically arrive within one to two weeks of your documents being complete — faster for straightforward cases.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. There's no cost to ask, and no cost to be assessed. Fees, if any, are agreed clearly before you commit to anything, and are usually taken from the financing amount itself — never billed to you separately.

What documents will I need?

At minimum: your company registration, the purchase order or sales contract, and shipping documents once available. We'll tell you exactly what applies to your case — nothing more.

Is this a loan? Will it show up as debt?

For the "already shipped" option, no — it's an advance on money your buyer already owes you, not a new loan. The working-capital and pre-shipment options do involve a facility from a bank or lender, and we'll always explain clearly which kind applies before you decide anything.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Egyptian exporters and importers with a real, verifiable trade — any sector, any size, as long as there's a genuine shipment or order behind it.

Is my information safe with you?

Yes. Your details are only shared with the specific bank or financing partner we introduce you to for your transaction, and only once you agree to move forward.
