Why work with us

We're not a bank, and we don't pretend to be one

Mutagarat doesn't lend money itself. Banks and licensed financiers make the credit decisions and provide the funds. We do the work in between.

  • We check eligibility before anything else. Registration, a genuine underlying shipment or order, an identifiable buyer, and a workable structure. If any of that is missing, we say so early rather than stringing you along.
  • We tell you exactly what's needed. A short list at first contact, and a fuller file only once your case moves toward a specific financier.
  • We already work with the financiers who fund these deals. Banks, licensed NBFIs, and international factors we have direct relationships with, not a cold introduction.

Common questions

Straight answers, before you ask

How long does the whole process take?+

It depends on how complete your documents are and how quickly the receiving bank or factor responds once we introduce you. The first step, checking whether your shipment or order clears our basic eligibility screen, is quick, since it's a short, fixed set of pass/fail questions. We'll give you a specific estimate for your case once we've looked at it, rather than a generic number that may not hold.

What documents will I need?+

At first contact, just the basics: your company name and sector, years trading, the confirmed order or shipment behind your request, typical ticket size, your buyer's or supplier's country, and any existing banking relationship. If it's a fit, we'll then ask for a fuller file: commercial registration and tax card, the purchase order or sales contract, shipping documents as they become available, recent financial statements or management accounts, and bank statements. We'll tell you exactly what applies to your case.

Is this a loan? Will it show up as debt?+

For the pre-shipment factoring option, no. It's an assignment of a receivable your buyer already owes you once the goods are loaded, not a new loan. The working-capital and pre-shipment financing options do involve a facility from a bank or licensed NBFI, and we'll always explain clearly which kind applies before you decide anything.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

Egyptian exporters and importers with a real, verifiable trade behind the request: a legally registered company, identifiable ownership, and a genuine shipment or confirmed order, not a paper transaction. We're not restricted to one sector, but we do turn away anything we can't verify.

Is my information safe with you?+

Your file is packaged and sent to one financier at a time. If we ever needed to share the same deal with more than one bank or factor at once, we'd tell you that first. It's not something we do by default.
